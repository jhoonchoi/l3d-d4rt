# T0 vs B0 / B0-C, and B0 ablations (OpenD4RT 48CLIP, clip [0, 48))

`scripts/l3d/run_t0.py --workers 8` (3136 s; code `l3d/t0.py`, `l3d/ensemble.py`). Same cells and partner outputs as `results/l3d/b0/` (k <= 6 from the existing dump).

- **T0** optimizes the outputs of one cell (the query's output and its `k - 1` partners: other source frames for source swap, other cameras mapped by `T*` for camera swap) to minimize `sum_j rho(|a - b_j| / z) + lam/2 (|a - a0|^2 + sum_j |b_j - b0_j|^2) / z^2`, with `u2` and `T*` held fixed, and scores the optimized query output. `rho`: `l2` (then T0 is a damped B0: the B0 mean as `lam -> 0`), `huber` or `cauchy` with the clip's median residual as scale (label-free). **Ray center**: the camera-0 outputs of queries with `t_s = t_t` move toward their lines through the frame's least-squares ray center (2 decodes per query, so compared with B0-C to cam 0 at k = 2 on the same kind of query).
- **Gain** = 1 - mean relative error `|P - X| / |X|` / that of the single output on the same cells (positive: helps). **vs B0** = 1 - error / B0-mean error on the same cells (positive: T0 beats B0). 95% CIs: 1000 resamples of each subset's clips.
- **T0 (CV)**: the penalty and `lam` are picked on one half of the clips (even / odd) and scored on the other, so the choice is not tuned on the clips it is scored on. **Hindsight**: the best setting on all clips (optimistic).
